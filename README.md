# Car Sales & Business Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Car Sales & Business Performance Dashboard** built with Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales activity, customer volume, geographical revenue distribution, vehicle color preferences, and monthly delivery charges.

The dashboard transforms sales data into an accessible visual reporting tool, enabling stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), explore sales patterns across markets, and identify areas for further business investigation.

## Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to help stakeholders:

- Monitor overall sales activity and customer volume.
- Evaluate revenue contribution across countries.
- Identify the most frequently sold vehicle colors.
- Track monthly delivery charges and fluctuations over time.
- Explore sales performance through interactive filters.
- Support data-driven commercial and operational decisions.

## Key Dashboard Insights

### 1. Geographical Sales Performance
The country-level sales visualization compares revenue across markets. The United Kingdom and the United States appear to be the leading markets in the displayed chart, followed by France and Switzerland.

### 2. Vehicle Color Preferences
The vehicle distribution chart shows red as the leading color, with 83 vehicles, followed by canary at 73 and blue at 60. These results provide a starting point for investigating customer preferences and potential inventory planning opportunities.

### 3. Monthly Delivery Charges
The monthly trend chart tracks delivery charges across the year. November records the highest displayed value at 31K, while August and September record 14K each. This variation could help stakeholders investigate seasonal logistics costs, delivery volumes, and operational efficiency.

### 4. Interactive Data Exploration
The country and color slicers allow users to filter the dashboard and examine performance across selected categories, making the report more useful for exploratory analysis.

*These observations reflect the values visible in the dashboard and should be validated against the underlying dataset before being used for business decisions.*
